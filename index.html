01 / ABOUT

Grounded in operations.
Built for the bigger picture.

I’m Kole Hunt, a cybersecurity consultant and technical solutions architect with a background in complex enterprise environments.

My experience at World Wide Technology spans security consulting, enterprise architecture, and global technical mentorship. Earlier work supporting Army endpoint management shaped my approach: understand the environment, make the tradeoffs explicit, and build solutions that can operate at scale.

I hold a B.S. in Information Technology – Security from Western Governors University. My professional background includes endpoint detection and response, data loss prevention, Tanium, and DoD risk management frameworks.
